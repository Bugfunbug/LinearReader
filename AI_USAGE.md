I am writing this document as I want to be as transparent as possible regarding my use of 
AI in this mod and why I decided to make this mod public.

I originally made this mod for private use. I had a small modded server with a couple of 
friends and the world size was getting quite big. After some research I found Xymb's
[Linear Region File Format](https://github.com/xymb-endcrystalme/LinearRegionFileFormatTools) 
and I wanted to make use of it on Forge. I cannot code, nor do I have the time to learn how to, 
so I used AI to make it for me.

When I saw that the mod worked quite well, I figured it might be fun to try and actually 
release a mod. I had never done any sort of coding project, so I figured it would be interesting 
to see what it is like. I never had any intention of making money off of LinearReader, and I never 
will. I am not enrolled in the CurseForge Rewards Program and I disabled monetization on Modrinth.
I don't really know how to prove that though, but if there's a way to I'd happily agree to do it.

The AIs that I have used are:
* Codex: Did most of the coding until recently.
* Claude: Has done the rest of the coding, basically.
* Gemini: Did a tiny bit of coding, and gave me some more technical ideas.
* ChatGPT: Makes sure the wiki and other things I write align with my actual code.

I only use "Free Plans", as I do not want to give these AI companies money. I used Codex until the 
usage for the free plan switched to reseting monthly, and currently I use Claude by sending ~1 
prompt every five hours.

LinearReader's logo, the static badges in the READMEs, the text of the READMEs, and most of the wiki 
were created/written by me. (For the wiki and READMEs, I did use ChatGPT to make sure that what I say 
is grammatically and technically correct). The non-static badges come from 
[LukenSkyne's Badges](https://github.com/LukenSkyne/Badges) on GitHub. I also used 
[Devin's Badges](https://intergrav.github.io/devins-badges-docs/badges/) for inspriation/reference.

In short:
* Code is 100% AI, but I check that it works as I intend.
* Wiki and READMEs are AI-assisted, but I did spend time writing them myself.
* Graphics are not AI generated.
* Only free plans were used, no money was given to AI companies.
* LinearReader is not monetized, nor will it ever be.
